🇧🇩 BRTA Official Refund & Cancellation Policy

As per the guidelines of the Bangladesh Road Transport Authority (BRTA) and the Passenger Rights Act, Jatra App BD follows a strict, transparent, and fully automated refund policy for ticket cancellations.

Cancellation Timeframe & Refund Percentage:
  • 🕒 48 Hours or more before departure 90% Refund
  • 🕒 Between 24 to 48 Hours before departure 50% Refund
  • 🕒 Between 12 to 24 Hours before departure 25% Refund
  • 🕒 Less than 12 Hours or after departure 0% (No Refund)
💡 Important Notes:
  • The 10% deduction on the 48-hour slot is kept as a standard system service charge.
  • Refunds are instantly processed back to your original payment method.
  • If you used Jatra Coins for booking, the used coins will be returned to your account.
  • Any Jatra Coins earned from the canceled trip will be deducted from your wallet.
